# Portfolio Growth Hub Guidelines

When working on marketing automation, analytics, and growth systems in BurtConsulting:

1. **Portfolio Memory & Context**:
   - BurtConsulting hosts the centralized **Portfolio Growth Hub** (`GROWTH_HUB.md`).
   - The Hub coordinates metrics and automated growth pipelines for James Burt's web apps: Bank of Gaga (`/Users/jamesburt/dev/loan-portal`), TeachingSax (`/Users/jamesburt/dev/TeachingSax`), TeachWeave (`/Users/jamesburt/dev/teach-weave`), etc.

2. **Headless Automation Over Screen Bots**:
   - Always prioritize API/webhook/database-level automation over screen-clicking or GUI automation.
   - Integrate directly with Stripe, GA4, Search Console, and platform APIs.

3. **High-Intent Over Generic Dropshipping**:
   - Do not generate generic e-commerce ad spam.
   - Growth focus must center on high-intent search (SEO problem-solvers), organic calculator tools, and authentic community engagement.
